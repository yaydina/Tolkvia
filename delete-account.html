How to delete your Tolkvia account and data

EnglishTürkçe

In the app

Open Settings → Delete account and confirm. This immediately:

  • deletes your account identifier, subscription status and usage counts from our server;
  • erases your history, favourites and settings from the device;
  • withdraws the app’s access to your Google or Apple account.

Without the app

Send an e-mail to info@stmatrix.com.tr with the subject “Delete my Tolkvia account”, from the e-mail address of your Google or Apple account. We delete the data listed above within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

What is kept

Nothing is kept on our server after deletion. Purchase records are held by Apple or Google under their own policies. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

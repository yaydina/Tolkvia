Who we are

Tolkvia is developed by StMatrix, the trade name of Yıldıray Aydın, an individual developer based in Kozlu, Zonguldak, Türkiye, who is the data controller. Contact: info@stmatrix.com.tr. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes and what choices you have.

Data that stays on your device

  • Text, speech and photos you translate while the language packs are installed (on-device translation).
  • Your translation history, favourites and settings.
  • Your name and e-mail address after you sign in, kept in the device’s secure storage.

This data is not sent to us. Deleting the app or your account removes it.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data for marketing, and no profiling.

Legal bases

Performance of a contract (providing translation, your account and your subscription) and legitimate interest (preventing abuse through usage and rate limits), under the GDPR and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK).

Retention

  • Account identifier and subscription status: until you delete your account.
  • Daily usage counts: deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Sign in with Apple token: until you delete your account, when it is revoked.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data and object to processing. Delete your account in Settings → Delete account or as described on the account deletion page, or write to info@stmatrix.com.tr. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (16 in the EU).

International transfers

Service providers may process data outside your country under appropriate safeguards, such as EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Changes

We will announce material changes in the app or on this page.
